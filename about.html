About North Star Bakery

A neighborhood baking spot

North Star began with a simple idea: a neighborhood should have a place to pick up fresh bread and feel welcome. Our small team makes familiar favorites and leaves room for seasonal ideas.

How we choose ingredients

We look for ingredients that bake well and taste good. When possible, we buy seasonal ingredients from nearby suppliers. Ask our team about ingredients before ordering if you have a food allergy.
